I build products that solve complex, real-world problems. My career began with hands-on product innovation projects and evolved into leading cross-functional teams delivering AI-enabled digital platforms in AgTech and EdTech.

With an MBA from Quantic University of Business and Technology and a Master of Science in Software Engineering, I bring a unique combination of business leadership and technical understanding to product management.
